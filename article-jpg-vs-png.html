JPG vs PNG: Which Image Format Should You Use?

Understand the main differences between JPG and PNG and choose the right format for your next image.

JPG and PNG are two of the most common image formats on the web. Both are useful, but they are designed for different situations. Choosing the right format can help you balance image quality, file size and transparency.

What is JPG?

JPG, also written JPEG, is a popular format for photographs and images with many colors and smooth transitions. JPG uses lossy compression, which means some image information can be removed to make the file smaller.

What is PNG?

PNG is designed for high-quality graphics and supports transparency. It uses lossless compression, so it is often a better choice when you need crisp edges, text, icons or a transparent background.

JPG vs PNG at a glance

  • JPG: usually better for photographs and smaller web image files.
  • PNG: often better for logos, screenshots, graphics, text and transparency.
  • JPG: does not support transparency.
  • PNG: supports transparent backgrounds.

When should you use JPG?

Use JPG when you are working with photographs or other detailed images where a smaller file size is important. JPG can be a practical choice for blog images, product photos and many social media graphics.

When should you use PNG?

PNG is useful when you need transparency or want to preserve sharp details. Logos, interface screenshots, diagrams and graphics containing text are common examples.

Can you make JPG and PNG files smaller?

Which format is better for websites?

There is no universal winner. For a photograph, JPG may provide a good balance between quality and file size. For an image that needs transparency or very sharp graphical details, PNG may be the better choice. The right decision depends on the content and how the image will be used.

Final thoughts

JPG and PNG both have a place on the web. Choose JPG for many photographs and situations where smaller files matter, and choose PNG when transparency or crisp graphical details are important.
