About the Word Counter

A word counter measures the length of text and can help you check whether writing fits a required word limit. This tool updates the word, character, sentence and estimated reading-time totals as you type or paste text. It is useful for essays, blog posts, applications, reports, product descriptions and social content. Word-count rules can differ between platforms, especially for hyphenated words, numbers and punctuation, so treat the result as a practical estimate and follow the rules of the place where you will submit your writing.

How to use this tool

  1. Tap or click inside the text box.
  2. Type your text or paste a draft from a document.
  3. Review the live totals for words, characters, sentences and estimated reading time.
  4. Edit the draft and watch the totals update until it fits your target.

Practical examples

  • Check whether a school essay is close to a 1,000-word assignment limit.
  • Estimate the length of a blog introduction before publishing.
  • Compare two product descriptions to see which one is shorter and easier to scan.

Tips for accurate results

Reading time is an estimate based on an average reading speed, not a promise about how long every person will take. For formal submissions, check whether headings, footnotes and references count toward the limit.

Frequently asked questions

How does this tool count words?

It splits non-empty text at whitespace, so consecutive spaces and line breaks do not create extra words.

Does it count characters with spaces?

Yes. The character total includes spaces and line breaks because it measures the text string length.

How is reading time estimated?

The displayed estimate uses approximately 200 words per minute and rounds up to a whole minute.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The counter runs in JavaScript on the page and does not send the text to an EasyWebTools server.
