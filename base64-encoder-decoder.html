TEXT TOOL

Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Encode text to Base64 or decode Base64 back to readable text. Arabic, emoji and other Unicode characters are supported with UTF-8, entirely in your browser.

How to use

  1. Enter your text or Base64 string in the input box.
  2. Choose Encode to convert text, or Decode to restore the original text.
  3. Use Copy to copy the result to your clipboard.
  4. Press Clear to reset both fields and start again.

Frequently asked questions

Does Base64 support Arabic text?

Yes. This tool uses UTF-8 conversion, so Arabic, emoji and other Unicode characters can be encoded and decoded.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Encoding and decoding are performed locally in your browser, with no external libraries or server upload.

What happens if the Base64 input is invalid?

The decoder validates the input and shows a clear error message if the string is malformed or cannot be decoded as valid UTF-8.
