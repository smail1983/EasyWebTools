Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth to calculate your current age.

About the Age Calculator

An age calculator compares a date of birth with the current date and expresses the elapsed time in years, months and days. It can be useful for a quick personal calculation, preparing a birthday reminder or checking an age range. Calendar months have different lengths, and leap years affect date arithmetic, so an age breakdown is not the same as dividing the number of days by 365. For legal eligibility, insurance, travel or official records, use the rules and reference date specified by the relevant authority.

How to use this tool

  1. Select your date of birth using the date field.
  2. Choose Calculate Age to compare the birth date with today's date on your device.
  3. Read the result in years, months and days.
  4. If the date is rejected, check that it is a real calendar date and is not in the future.

Practical examples

  • Find the age of a person born on a known date for a birthday card.
  • Check how old a child is in years and months before completing a general form.
  • Calculate an approximate age breakdown for a personal milestone or event.

Tips for accurate results

The result depends on your device's current date and local calendar. Time zones can affect the calendar date around midnight. Official age rules may use a specific jurisdiction, time zone or inclusive counting convention.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator show years, months and days?

Yes. It subtracts calendar components and adjusts for the length of the previous month when needed.

Does it account for leap years?

It uses JavaScript calendar dates, which include leap-year calendar behavior.

Can I enter a future date?

No. A date later than the current date is rejected as an invalid birth date.

Is the date of birth uploaded?

No. The date is read and compared in the browser and is not submitted to an EasyWebTools server.
