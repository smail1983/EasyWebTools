How to Count Characters Online

Check characters, spaces and lines instantly when your text has a character limit.

Character limits are common in forms, social posts, titles, descriptions and online applications. A character counter makes it easy to check your text before you submit or publish it.

What is a character counter?

A character counter counts the characters in a piece of text. Depending on the tool, it can also show words, spaces, lines and other useful statistics.

How to count characters online

  1. Open the EasyWebTools Character Counter.
  2. Paste or type your text.
  3. Review the character total.
  4. Edit your text until it fits the required limit.

Do spaces count as characters?

It depends on the limit and the platform. Many character limits count spaces and punctuation, so it is useful to check the exact rules of the service where you plan to use the text.

Character count vs word count

Character count measures individual characters, while word count measures groups of characters separated into words. A text can have a low word count but still exceed a character limit if the words are long.

When is a character counter useful?

  • Writing short social media posts.
  • Preparing page titles and descriptions.
  • Completing application forms with strict limits.
  • Checking short messages and advertisements.

Final thoughts

Checking your character count before publishing can prevent unnecessary edits later. Use the free Character Counter whenever you need a quick, accurate count.
