About the Text Case Converter

A text case converter changes the capitalization of existing text without requiring you to retype it. This tool provides uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case transformations. It is useful for cleaning pasted text, standardizing headings, preparing labels and correcting inconsistent capitalization. Case conversion changes letters, not the meaning or accuracy of the words, so review names, abbreviations, product codes and language-specific capitalization after applying a transformation.

How to use this tool

  1. Paste or type the text you want to change into the text area.
  2. Choose UPPERCASE to capitalize all letters, or lowercase to convert all letters to small forms.
  3. Choose Title Case for headline-style capitalization, or Sentence case for sentence openings.
  4. Review the result carefully, then copy it into your document or publishing tool.

Practical examples

  • Convert a heading typed in all caps into normal sentence case.
  • Standardize a list of labels or category names before importing them into a spreadsheet.
  • Change a pasted paragraph to lowercase before applying your own formatting.

Tips for accurate results

Title case conventions vary by style guide. Some guides keep short articles and prepositions lowercase, while this simple converter capitalizes word starts. Always review formal headlines and proper nouns.

Frequently asked questions

What case styles are available?

The tool supports uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case.

Does it change punctuation or numbers?

No. It changes letter capitalization and leaves punctuation and numbers in place.

Will title case follow every publishing style guide?

No. It uses a simple word-start capitalization rule, so check short words and brand names manually.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. The transformations use JavaScript string methods in your browser.
