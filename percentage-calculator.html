About the Percentage Calculator

A percentage expresses a value as a part of 100. A percentage calculator helps answer common questions such as finding 15 percent of a price, measuring a discount or comparing an old value with a new one. This page includes a calculation for X percent of Y and a percentage-change calculation. The result is mathematical guidance; for invoices, taxes, interest or regulated financial decisions, confirm the applicable rules and rounding method before relying on a figure.

How to use this tool

  1. For a percentage of a number, enter the percentage in the first field.
  2. Enter the number in the second field and choose Calculate.
  3. For percentage change, enter the original value and the new value in the second calculator.
  4. Choose Calculate change and read the signed result: positive means an increase, negative means a decrease.

Practical examples

  • Find 15% of $200 to calculate a simple discount amount ($30).
  • Compare a monthly bill that changed from $80 to $92; the increase is 15%.
  • Calculate a 20% reduction from a $50 starting price; the reduction amount is $10.

Tips for accurate results

Percentage change is calculated relative to the original value. The original value cannot be zero because division by zero is undefined. A percentage-point difference is not the same as a percentage change.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find X percent of Y?

Multiply Y by X and divide the result by 100.

How is percentage increase calculated?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, then multiply by 100.

Why can't the original value be zero?

Percentage change uses the original value as its denominator, and division by zero is undefined.

Does the calculator round the result?

The percentage-change display is rounded to two decimal places; the first calculation displays up to ten fractional digits.
