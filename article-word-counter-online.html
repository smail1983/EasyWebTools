How to Count Words in an Essay Online

Check word count, character count and reading time quickly in your browser.

Word count is useful when writing essays, blog posts, applications, reports and social media content. Instead of counting words manually, an online word counter can give you an instant result.

Why does word count matter?

Many assignments and publishing platforms have recommended or required word limits. Knowing your word count helps you stay within the requested range and keep your writing focused.

How to count words online

  1. Open the EasyWebTools Word Counter.
  2. Paste or type your text into the editor.
  3. Check the displayed word count and character count.
  4. Review the reading-time estimate when useful.

Word count vs character count

Word count measures the number of words in your text, while character count measures individual letters, numbers, punctuation marks and other characters. Some forms and social platforms use character limits rather than word limits.

Tips for staying within a word limit

  • Remove repeated ideas and unnecessary phrases.
  • Use clear sentences instead of adding filler.
  • Check the word count after your final edits.
  • Keep a small margin below a strict maximum limit.

Final thoughts

A word counter is a simple tool that can save time and make it easier to meet writing requirements. Try the free Word Counter whenever you need a quick count.
