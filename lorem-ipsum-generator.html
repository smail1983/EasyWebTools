Lorem Ipsum Generator

Create placeholder content for mockups, layouts, prototypes and design previews. Choose paragraphs, sentences or words, then copy your generated text.

How to use

  1. Select whether you want paragraphs, sentences or words.
  2. Enter the amount you need, within the displayed limit.
  3. Click Generate to create placeholder text.
  4. Click Copy to copy the text for your mockup or draft.

Frequently asked questions

What is Lorem Ipsum used for?

Lorem Ipsum is placeholder text used to preview layouts, typography and content spacing before final copy is ready.

Can I generate words, sentences or paragraphs?

Yes. Select your preferred format and choose the amount to generate within the tool's limits.

Does this generator send my text to a server?

No. The generator runs in JavaScript in your browser and does not upload generated text.
