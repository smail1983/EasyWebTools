About the Lorem Ipsum Generator

A Lorem Ipsum generator creates placeholder text that helps designers and developers preview a layout before final copy is ready. It is useful for checking typography, spacing, cards, page templates and prototypes without writing temporary marketing copy. This generator can produce paragraphs, sentences or individual words in a chosen amount. The result is sample filler text, not meaningful editorial content, and should be replaced before a design or page is published.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose Paragraphs, Sentences or Words from the content type selector.
  2. Enter the amount you need, staying within the maximum displayed for that content type.
  3. Select Generate to create placeholder copy in the output field.
  4. Select Copy to move the generated text into your design, document or prototype.

Practical examples

  • Fill a blog article template to test heading spacing and paragraph width.
  • Preview a card grid with several paragraphs of different lengths.
  • Check how a mobile page wraps text before the final product description is written.

Tips for better results

Use realistic text lengths when testing a layout. A page that looks balanced with filler copy may behave differently when real headings, links, lists and longer words are inserted.
